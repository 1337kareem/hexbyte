How to duel

  1. Hold a gesture for half a second: ✊ fire, ✋ shield, ✌️ lightning. Player 1 stands on the left.
  2. Say an incantation. The AI rates its creativity from 1 to 10, and power sets the damage.
  3. Mana is limited. Prune counts your spells and stops you when you run out.
  4. No cheating. Incantations that try to rewrite the rules get blocked by Prune and backfire.

Keyboard: Player 1 uses 1 2 3, Player 2 uses 8 9 0 (fire, shield, lightning).

Combat log

Every spell has a receipt signed by Prune. Verify it, or tamper with it and watch it fail.
