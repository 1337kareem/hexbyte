  1. Hold the gesture for half a second and say an incantation. An orb appears in your hand. Player 1 stands on the left.
  2. Throw it by swinging your arm at your opponent (or press the spell key again). Headshots do 1.5×, arms and legs 0.75×.
  3. Dodge: jump over low throws, duck under high ones (keys A/S and J/K), or pop a shield.
  4. The AI judge rates your incantation from 1 to 10 a few seconds later. A creative one powers up your orb.
  5. Spam attacks. Throw and cast again as fast as you like. Mana is how many spells the AI judge scores through Prune; after that, attacks fly at base power. Only the shield has a cooldown.
  6. No cheating. Incantations that try to rewrite the rules get blocked by Prune and backfire.

Keyboard mode: Player 1 uses 1 2 3 Q (jump A, duck S), Player 2 uses 8 9 0 U (jump J, duck K). Press your spell key again to throw.
